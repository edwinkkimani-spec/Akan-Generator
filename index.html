The Akan Name Generator

In Akan culture, every child is given a name based on the day of the week they were born, known as a "day name" or "kradin." These names carry deep meaning and are believed to influence a person's character and destiny. Our Akan Name Generator lets you discover your own traditional day name simply by entering your date of birth and gender. Whether you're exploring your heritage, curious about Ghanaian traditions, or just having fun, this tool connects you to a centuries-old naming custom celebrated across the Akan people of Ghana and Ivory Coast.
